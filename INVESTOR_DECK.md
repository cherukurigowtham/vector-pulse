## Slide 2: Problem
**Title**
COD is still creating expensive avoidable loss

**Core points**
- D2C merchants ship COD orders that were never likely to convert.
- Fake orders and repeated address abuse create avoidable RTO costs.
- Manual review does not scale during campaign spikes.
- Blanket prepaid rules hurt conversion because good buyers get blocked too.

**Suggested visual**
[02.svg](/Users/gowthamcherukuri/Desktop/vector_pulse/investor_deck/assets/02.svg)

---

## Slide 3: Why Now
**Title**
Why this matters now

**Core points**
- Indian D2C brands are under stronger profitability pressure.
- COD still matters for conversion, so merchants cannot simply turn it off.
- Smaller brands lack focused tools for COD-specific risk control.

**Suggested visual**
[03.svg](/Users/gowthamcherukuri/Desktop/vector_pulse/investor_deck/assets/03.svg)

---

## Slide 4: Solution
**Title**
Vector Pulse turns risky order flow into a clear operational decision

**Core points**
- Score each order in real time
- Return `ALLOW_COD` or `FORCE_PREPAID`
- Explain why an order was flagged
- Tune the risk profile by merchant

## Slide 5: Product Flow
**Title**
How the product works

**Flow**
